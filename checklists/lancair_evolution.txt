# Lancair Evolution (Laminar Research, N844X) - from the Checklists section of
# X-Plane_Lancair_Evo_Pilot_Operating_Manual.pdf (p.44-62), which ships inside
# the aircraft folder. The POM says of itself: "customized to the X-Plane
# Lancair Evolution. These differ from those of the real aircraft", and its
# start list is "a sub-set of the real procedures".
#
# Where this file departs from the POM, and why:
# - The condition lever is LOW IDLE (the middle) for flight, not the POM's
#   "FULL FORWARD (HIGH IDLE)" after start. The real N844X POH: "full forward
#   is high idle, which we do not use in this plane; middle is low idle, where
#   we set that lever for flight", and the profile's condition_run is 0.5.
# - The POM has no Taxi, Climb or Descent lists. Taxi, Climb and Descent below
#   are built from the POM's After Takeoff and Cruise lists plus the POH's
#   handling notes (taxi in beta, not on the brakes; 1,250 ft-lb takeoff
#   torque; cruise prop 1,700; 10 gallons at most of fuel imbalance).
# - The POM's 25 degree takeoff flap is the 50% detent (20 degrees), the
#   nearest the aircraft has; its "50 degrees" landing flap is read as 50% or
#   more, the pilot's pick.
# - The POM's Parking list is SHUTDOWN here.
#
# Conditions are given ONLY on stock datarefs whose value sense is certain.
# Anything else (prop lever, ignition, bleed air, door seals, cabin dump, the
# condition lever during the start, the door) is a call-out: the pilot's item.
# Condition-lever and landing-gear reads are CHECK items, so they are never
# written: the gear goes through its own gated tool, never a raw handle write.
